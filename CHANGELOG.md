# Changelog

All notable changes to Nodepad are documented here.

## [0.1.0] - 2025-07-27

### Added

- **Thinking Workspace** — A self-contained map of one topic, with Notes, Labels, and Relationships.
- **Note types** — Classify Notes as claim, question, idea, reference, or thesis.
- **Persistent capture bar** — Quickly capture thoughts from anywhere in the app.
- **Inline editing** — Edit any Note's text, type, labels, and annotations in place.
- **Canvas placement** — Position Notes freely on the workspace canvas.
- **Relationship drawing** — Draw meaningful associations between Notes on the canvas.
- **Focus & dim** — Focus on one Note and dim the rest to reduce cognitive load.
- **View switcher** — Switch between list, kanban, and canvas views.
- **Kanban drag** — Drag Notes between type columns in kanban view.
- **Background enrichment** — AI suggests labels, annotations, and relationships for your Notes.
- **Suggested Relationships** — AI proposes connections between Notes; accept or dismiss inline.
- **Synthesis** — AI proposes provisional insights that connect tensions across multiple Notes.
- **Workspace rail & switcher** — Switch between multiple Thinking Workspaces.
- **Command palette** — ⌘K palette for quick actions, with expandable sections.
- **Workspace settings** — Per-workspace AI assistance policy and model selection.
- **Cloud AI providers** — Optional Ollama Cloud and compatible providers with keychain-stored credentials.
- **Backup & restore** — Automatic daily backups, manual archive export/import, and restore from backup.
- **macOS interactions** — Full keyboard navigation, accessibility support, and external link handling.
- **Privacy-first design** — No telemetry, no server, no browser storage. AI is opt-in per workspace.
